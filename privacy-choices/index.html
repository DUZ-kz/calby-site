In-app controls available today

  • Edit preferences: update profile details and goals inside the app.
  • Reminder settings: adjust notification preferences from the Profile screen.
  • Reset my data: clear synced profile and app-state data for the signed-in account and remove related local data on the current device.
  • Delete account: permanently remove the Calby account, synced data, and local files tied to that account on the current device.
  • Sign out: remove the current session from the device.

Account deletion without the app

If you cannot access Calby, email calby.support@gmail.com with the subject "Calby Account Deletion Request" and include the email address connected to your account.

Calby may ask for limited verification before deleting account data to protect accounts from unauthorized requests.

Requests that should go through support

  • Account deletion follow-up requests
  • Questions about synced data retention
  • Questions about AI scan processing
  • Requests related to privacy laws that apply in your region

How to request help: email calby.support@gmail.com or use the hosted support page.

Product library and habit controls, where available

In versions with the product analyzer, you can remove saved product assessments or favorites, review allergen preferences, and choose whether to refresh an assessment from its sources. Habit settings let you change a weekly goal, pause participation or hide habit progress. Hiding habit progress does not itself delete account data or disable all analytics.

Reset and account deletion also clear the corresponding private product labels, history, feedback and habit preferences from active account records. Deleting underlying activity can change derived habit points. See the Privacy Policy for retention exceptions and backup expiry.

Current data model note

Profile information, synced app state, and backed-up body progress photos can be restored after sign-in on a new phone. See the Privacy Policy for Calby's data retention and deletion practices.
